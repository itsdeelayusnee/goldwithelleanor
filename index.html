My Gold Journey

My journey with Public Gold started as a saver. I was first introduced to gold saving in 2016, but at that time I associated gold mainly with jewellery and decided it was not for me.

In 2022, I began looking at gold differently — as one of the ways I could preserve the value of my hard-earned money over the long term. I started small with 1g and continued learning, saving and accumulating consistently.

Over time, I converted part of my cash-saving challenges into gold and built my personal holdings to 229.6792g. What began as a personal financial journey gradually became a platform for education and guidance.

Why I Built Gold with Elleanor

Gold saving can feel complicated at the beginning, especially when dealing with changing prices, different currencies, product sizes, fees, targets and zakat.

I built this website to bring the tools my gold savers frequently need into one place — so they can learn, calculate and track their progress more easily.

  • Check Public Gold prices and estimated BND values
  • Estimate how many grams a selected budget may purchase
  • Track gold accumulation and saving targets
  • View annual gold-saving projections
  • Estimate Brunei gold zakat
  • Access registration and first-purchase guidance

Dealer Approach

My approach is education first, not pressure. I share what I have learned from my own gold-saving journey and guide new, existing and returning savers through the practical steps of understanding Public Gold, GAP and physical gold.

This page is designed to support informed decisions and consistent saving according to each person’s own financial ability.
